Services

Our Core Services

Mobile diesel repair, fleet maintenance and mechanic staffing, built for Houston's trucking industry.

1. 24/7 Mobile Diesel Dispatch

When a truck breaks down, every minute counts. We dispatch certified, independent mobile diesel technicians directly to your location, equipped for roadside emergencies and on-site repairs to get your rig back on the road fast.

  • Electrical & System Diagnostics
  • Brake System Repairs & Air Leak Fixes
  • Hose, Belt, & Sensor Replacements
  • Fuel System & Engine Component Troubleshooting
  • Emergency Roadside Service Dispatch

2. On-Site Fleet Maintenance Coordination

Avoid unnecessary shop trips and tow fees. We connect your fleet with trusted local mechanics who perform routine servicing right at your facility or yard, keeping your trucks compliant, safe, and efficient.

  • PM (Preventative Maintenance) Services & Oil Changes
  • DOT Inspections & Pre-Trip Prep
  • Fluid Level Checks & Filter Replacements
  • Tire Inspection & Maintenance

3. Fleet Mechanic Partnering & Staffing

Scaling your fleet or running short on in-house maintenance cover? We link trucking companies with dedicated, fully vetted 3rd-party diesel mechanics tailored to your specific operational needs.

  • Vetted, On-Call Independent Technicians
  • Customized Fleet Service Agreements
  • Flexible Scheduling for Large or Small Fleets

About

Keeping Houston's fleets moving

In the trucking industry, downtime isn't just an inconvenience, it's money lost. Founded right here in Greater Houston, our mission is simple: keep your truck on the road, your fleet running efficiently, and your business moving forward.

We bridge the gap between fleet owners, drivers, and vetted mobile diesel mechanics. Instead of waiting hours for a tow or losing days at a traditional shop, we dispatch trusted third-party technicians, fully vetted and covered with verified insurance, wherever and whenever you need us.

  • 24/7 Mobile Diesel Repair

    Emergency roadside assistance and on-site diagnostics so you can get back to hauling without wasted miles.

  • Vetted & Insured Network

    Peace of mind knowing every third-party technician dispatched to your vehicle meets strict safety, skill, and insurance standards.

  • Preventative Fleet Maintenance

    Custom service schedules, oil changes, and full inspections designed to catch issues before they cause costly breakdowns.

  • We Come to You

    No waiting hours for a tow or losing days at a shop. Technicians are dispatched straight to your location or yard.
